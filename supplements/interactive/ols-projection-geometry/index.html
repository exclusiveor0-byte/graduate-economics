① 관측공간 직교사영

$\hat{y} = P_X y \in \mathcal{C}(X)$
$\hat{e} = M_X y = y - \hat{y} \perp \mathcal{C}(X)$
정상방정식: $X'\hat{e} = \begin{pmatrix} \mathbf{1}'\hat{e} \\ x'\hat{e} \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$
종속변수 $\|y\|^2$ 21.000
적합값 $\|\hat{y}\|^2$ (ESS uncentered) 18.333 (55/3)
잔차제곱합 $\|\hat{e}\|^2$ (RSS) 2.667 (8/3)
직교성 확인 $y'y - (\|\hat{y}\|^2 + \|\hat{e}\|^2)$ 0.0000 (Exact)
중심화 총제곱합 $TSS = \|y - \bar{y}\mathbf{1}\|^2$ 4.667 (14/3)
중심화 설명제곱합 $ESS = \|\hat{y} - \bar{y}\mathbf{1}\|^2$ 2.000 (6/3)
결정계수 $R^2 = ESS / TSS$ 0.4286 (3/7)
